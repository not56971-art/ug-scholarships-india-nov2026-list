🎓 Recent Undergraduate Scholarships in India

Compiled from sources published in the last 7 days (approx. Nov 1–8, 2026). Always verify on official portals.

Disclaimer: Information is based on recent news articles and aggregator sites (Buddy4Study, etc.). Deadlines and details can change. Confirm eligibility, amounts, and application process on the official website before applying. Many applications are via Buddy4Study (b4s.in links).

1. ONGC Merit Scholarship 2026-27

Amount: ₹48,000 per annum (till course completion)
Application Deadline: November 30, 2026
Eligibility: First-year students from SC, ST, OBC, and economically weaker General categories pursuing Engineering (4 yrs), MBBS, MBA, or Master’s in Geology/Geophysics. Indian nationals under 31 years, ≥60% marks or 6.0 CGPA. Family income: SC/ST < ₹4.5 lakh; OBC/Gen < ₹2 lakh. 50% seats for girls. Apply via ongcscholar.org (after NSP OTR).
Apply Link: www.b4s.in/nnes/ONGM1 or official ONGC portal

2. Vylor Agritech Scholarship Programme FY 2026–27

Amount: ₹25,000 per year (UG); ₹35,000 (PG/PhD)
Application Deadline: November 30, 2026
Eligibility: Students in any year of UG (Agriculture & allied) at IITs, NITs, IISc, ICAR-recognised colleges, or State Agricultural Universities. Annual family income ≤ ₹6 lakh. Preference to female students. Not for children of Vylor/Buddy4Study employees.
Apply Link: www.b4s.in/nnes/VASP1

3. SDEF India Scholarship Program 2026-27

Amount: Up to ₹50,000 per year (renewable up to 4 years; total up to ₹2 lakh)
Application Deadline: November 30, 2026 (confirm as some earlier reports said Oct 31)
Eligibility: First-year full-time professional UG: B.Tech/B.E., MBBS, BDS, B.Arch. Family income ≤ ₹12 lakh. No gap/drop year after Class 12. Min 80% in Class 12 (or category-specific). Meet entrance rank criteria (e.g., JEE Main AIR ≤50,000 etc.). Disbursed to college.
Apply Link: www.b4s.in/nnes/SDEF1 or swamidayanand.org

4. Satya Scholarship Program 2026

Amount: 100% annual course fee + hostel/accommodation/meals support
Application Deadline: November 15, 2026 (recent reports; some earlier said Oct 31)
Eligibility: First-year in 3-5 year UG/integrated in Medical & Health Sciences (MBBS, BDS, BPT, B.Sc. Nursing, etc.). From Punjab (selected institutes in Ludhiana) or Ludhiana district to top NIRF medical institutes. Class 12 ≥60% from Punjab board. Parental income ≤ ₹8 lakh. Indian citizens, no other scholarship. Preference: girls, sports, PwD, orphans, single parents.
Apply Link: www.b4s.in/nnes/SAT4

5. WINGS – Women INspiring Growth in STEM Scholarship Programme

Amount: ₹2 lakh per annum (tuition fee support)
Application Deadline: November 30, 2026 (recent; earlier reports Oct 31)
Eligibility: Female students planning UG STEM course at IIT Bombay for 2027–28 session. Not availing any other government tuition grant/scholarship. Maintain min CPI 6.0 for continuation. Initiative by Bharat Desai & Neerja Sethi.
Apply Link: www.b4s.in/nnes/WINGS2

6. Mahindra Saarthi Abhiyaan 2026-27

Amount: ₹10,000 fixed award
Application Deadline: November 30, 2026
Eligibility: Girls (daughters/wards of truck drivers) studying in Class 11, 12, Graduation (UG), or Post-Graduation across India. Not for children of Buddy4Study or Mahindra employees.
Apply Link: www.b4s.in/nnes/MHSA4

7. SML Mahindra Scholarship Program 2026-27

Amount: Up to ₹30,000 (category-based)
Application Deadline: November 15, 2026
Eligibility: Girls who are daughters of drivers of heavy/light commercial vehicles (goods/passenger). For Class 11-12 or UG/diploma/polytechnic in STEM & core fields (any year). Min 60% marks, family income ≤ ₹6 lakh. Categories: SML Saarthi and SML Unnati.
Apply Link: www.b4s.in/nnes/SMLS1
